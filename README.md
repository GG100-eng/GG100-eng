## Selected builds

### [AI Manager](https://github.com/GG100-eng/AI-manager)

An interactive product-review prototype. Explore category scores, the reasoning behind them, and suggested fixes across different review scenarios.

- **Product question:** How can feedback help someone revise their work?
- **Built with:** Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- **Status:** Scripted demo scenarios. Live document analysis and model evaluation are future work.

[Try the demo](https://ai-manager-delta.vercel.app/) · [Read the product decisions](https://github.com/GG100-eng/AI-manager#product-choices-in-the-prototype)

### [Heimdall](https://github.com/GG100-eng/heimdall)

A finite feed organised around what matters right now. Posts are grouped into now, serendipity and later, and the reading session has an end.

- **Product question:** Can a shorter, goal-focused feed make useful information easier to act on?
- **Built with:** React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS.
- **Status:** Working reader with prepared feed snapshots and local interactions. The AI-ranking evaluation is a plan, not a published benchmark.

[Try the demo](https://heimdall-aae.pages.dev/) · [Read the evaluation plan](https://github.com/GG100-eng/heimdall/blob/main/docs/evaluation-plan.md)

### [Local AI Health Companion](https://github.com/GG100-eng/Local-AI-Health)

A browser-based experiment with local model inference, a symptom journal and a PDF handoff the user can inspect before sharing.

- **Product question:** What can a useful health-information companion keep on the device?
- **Built with:** TypeScript, Transformers.js, WebGPU/WASM and IndexedDB.
- **Status:** Experimental prototype. Use fictional information to explore it; it is not medical advice.

[Explore the implementation](https://github.com/GG100-eng/Local-AI-Health#explore-the-implementation)
